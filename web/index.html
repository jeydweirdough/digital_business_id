Getmeds Card

Share your business card with a QR code.

Android

Your phone

The full app: QR code, plus sharing by tapping phones.

Download for Android
  1. Tap Download for Android.
  2. Open the downloaded file GetmedsCard.apk.
  3. If your phone asks, allow installing apps from this source, then tap Install.
  4. Open Getmeds Card and sign in.

iPhone

Your phone

Free from this page, no App Store needed. Shares by QR code.

Get it on iPhone
  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap Get it on iPhone.
  3. Tap the Share button , then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open Getmeds Card from your home screen and sign in.

Sign in with the email on your business card.
First time? Tap First time / Forgot password? on the sign-in screen.
